DealerOS AI

The dealership platform that reads your paperwork for you. DealerOS AI is a management tool for independent car dealerships. It reads bills of sale, auction invoices and payoff letters, pulls out the important details, and fills out the forms you need to file — plus keeps track of inventory, sales, accounting and marketing, all in one place.

What it does

Reads your documents

Reads bills of sale, auction invoices, lender payoff letters and purchase contracts. Works with scanned pages and photos, not just clean digital files, and can handle a whole batch of documents at once.

Double-checks the vehicle

Every vehicle ID number is checked for errors and looked up in the official government database, so the make, model and year on your record match the real vehicle — not just whatever the paperwork said.

Fills out the forms

Used-vehicle records and bills of sale are filled in automatically from the details it read — no retyping, and no mismatch between what you filed and what you kept on record.

Photo Studio

Drop in a photo of a car on your lot and it swaps the background for a clean studio backdrop. It works right in your browser, so photos are never uploaded anywhere, and it even works without an internet connection.

Inventory & sales

Track vehicles from the moment you buy them, through repairs, to the sale — with costs, repairs and profit tracked for each one.

Post listings everywhere

Write a listing once and publish it across Facebook Marketplace, Instagram, TikTok, Craigslist, YouTube Shorts, Google and your own site — and see which one actually brought in each lead.

Accounting & expenses

Track business and advertising costs, cash flow, and how much profit each vehicle made, across your whole lot.

Customers

Customer records with notes, documents, insurance policies and warranty contracts, all kept together under the buyer they belong to.

Compliance records

Registry and compliance paperwork with a full history of changes, so the paper trail behind each vehicle stays intact.

Team management

Staff roles, attendance tracking, performance reports and a built-in messaging channel for your team.

Demand forecasting

Predicts what will sell based on your inventory, and lets dealerships on the platform swap vehicles between lots to move stock where it'll sell fastest.

Multiple dealerships

Run any number of dealerships from one account, each with its own data, branding and staff — with four levels of access, from lot staff up to the owner.
