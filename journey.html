Take a working idea and make it production-ready

It works as a demo — now it needs to hold up with real traffic, real data and real customers. We strengthen it, test it thoroughly, and launch it properly.

  1. Review what you have now and find where it could break
  2. Add testing, safety checks and ongoing monitoring
  3. Rebuild the parts that need to be faster or handle more load
  4. Roll it out gradually and track how well it performs

Add an AI feature without breaking what already works

You have a product and real users already. We add AI as one feature at a time — designed, tested, and easy to undo — not a risky overhaul of everything.

  1. Find the one feature that would help the most, and plan around it safely
  2. Build a working version inside your existing product
  3. Test it with a small group before rolling it out to everyone
  4. Document everything and hand it over clean

Get a stalled AI project moving again

The demo looked great, then progress stopped. We find out what's actually holding it back — the data, the testing, the setup, or just too much scope — and get it launched.

  1. A quick review to find exactly what's blocking the launch
  2. Trim the scope down to a version we can ship now
  3. Fix whatever gap is actually holding it back
  4. Launch it, measure the results, then keep improving it
